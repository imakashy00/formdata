Static websites hosted on platforms like GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or AWS S3 are fast and resilient. But because they do not have a server backend, you cannot run PHP scripts or database queries to process contact forms.

Formdata provides a hosted endpoint for your HTML form. You simply create a form endpoint URL in Formdata, paste it into your HTML <form action="...">, and Formdata handles everything: submission capture, spam filtering, email alerts, and live synchronization to Google Sheets.

1 Get Your Form Endpoint URL

Before writing any HTML, you need your unique Formdata endpoint:

  1. Visit Formdata.cloud and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click New Form on your dashboard.
  3. Give your form a name (e.g., "Portfolio Contact Form" or "Static Site Leads").
  4. Your form is created instantly with a dedicated public endpoint URL in this format: https://formdata.cloud/f/YOUR_FORM_ID
Pro Tip: You do not need to pre-define fields or configure database tables in Formdata. Formdata dynamically inspects and captures whatever inputs you send it!

2 Connect the Endpoint to Your HTML Form

Open your static HTML file (e.g. index.html or contact.html). Locate or create your <form> tag, and set the following two mandatory attributes:

  • action="https://formdata.cloud/f/YOUR_FORM_ID" (replace with your real endpoint ID)
  • method="POST"
HTML 
<form action="https://formdata.cloud/f/YOUR_FORM_ID" method="POST">
  <!-- Form inputs will go here -->
  <button type="submit">Send Message</button>
</form>

3 Add Form Fields with Proper name Attributes

Formdata turns every input's name attribute into a column header in your submission logs and synced Google Sheets.

Always choose clear, descriptive names such as name="name", name="email", name="message", or name="company":

HTML 
<!-- Full Name -->
<label for="name">Your Name</label>
<input type="text" id="name" name="name" placeholder="Alex Morgan" required>

<!-- Email Address (used for autoresponders) -->
<label for="email">Email Address</label>
<input type="email" id="email" name="email" placeholder="alex@example.com" required>

<!-- Custom Field: Inquiry Type -->
<label for="subject">Subject</label>
<select id="subject" name="subject">
  <option value="General Inquiry">General Inquiry</option>
  <option value="Project Quote">Project Quote</option>
  <option value="Support">Support</option>
</select>

<!-- Message Box -->
<label for="message">Message</label>
<textarea id="message" name="message" rows="4" placeholder="Tell us about your project..." required></textarea>
Important: Ensure you include a field named email. Formdata automatically detects this field to power autoresponder emails and deliverability tracking.

4 Add Zero-Friction Honeypot Spam Protection

Spam bots scan websites for forms and automatically fill out every single field they can find. Formdata features an integrated honeypot detector that rejects bots without showing annoying puzzle captchas to real humans.

Simply add an invisible input field named _gotcha (or your custom configured honeypot name) inside your form:

Invisible Honeypot 
<!-- Formdata Honeypot Spam Protection -->
<input type="text" name="_gotcha" tabindex="-1" autocomplete="off" 
       style="position: absolute; left: -9999px; width: 1px; height: 1px; opacity: 0;">

How this works: Real human users cannot see or tab into this field, so it remains empty. Automated spam scripts automatically populate it with junk values. When Formdata sees a value in _gotcha, it silently drops the spam submission, protecting your inbox.

5 (Optional) Add Cloudflare Turnstile Bot Defense

If your website experiences high-traffic or aggressive bot campaigns, you can enable enterprise-grade Cloudflare Turnstile protection.

  1. In your Formdata form settings, enable Turnstile Protection and enter your Cloudflare Turnstile Secret Key.
  2. Include Cloudflare's script once in your HTML <head> or right before your form:
    HTML
    <script src="https://challenges.cloudflare.com/turnstile/v0/api.js" async defer></script>
  3. Add the Turnstile widget element inside your <form>:
    HTML
    <div class="cf-turnstile" data-sitekey="YOUR_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY"></div>

Formdata automatically verifies the submitted token with Cloudflare's servers, rejecting fraudulent requests with zero backend code on your end.

6 Connect Real-Time Google Sheets Synchronization

One of Formdata's most popular superpowers is instant, real-time Google Sheets synchronization.

Instead of paying for expensive Zapier or Make subscriptions to pipe data into a spreadsheet:

  1. In your Formdata dashboard, navigate to your form's Integrations tab.
  2. Click Connect Google Sheets and authorize your Google account.
  3. Select an existing Google Sheet or let Formdata create a brand-new spreadsheet automatically.

Every time a visitor clicks "Send Message" on your static HTML website, Formdata appends a new row to your Google Sheet with timestamp, name, email, and message within milliseconds.

7 Configure Email Notifications & Autoresponders

Keep your team and your visitors informed as soon as a form is submitted:

  • Instant Owner Notifications: By default, Formdata delivers clean email summaries of every submission to your account email address. You can add extra team notification emails in settings.
  • Automated Visitor Autoresponder: Enable the autoresponder in Formdata to send an instant, customized confirmation message back to the visitor's email address (e.g., "Thanks for reaching out! We received your message and will reply within 24 hours.").

8 Deploy Your Static Website & Test Live

Once your HTML file is ready, deploy it to your static web host:

  • GitHub Pages: Push your HTML file to a GitHub repository and turn on GitHub Pages in repository settings.
  • Netlify / Vercel: Drag and drop your folder or connect your Git repository for instant CDN deployment.
  • Cloudflare Pages: Connect your repository for fast worldwide edge distribution.

Testing Your Live Form

Visit your live deployed site, fill out your contact form with test data, and hit submit:

  1. You will see Formdata's clean, branded confirmation page (or your custom configured redirect URL).
  2. Check your email inbox for the instant notification.
  3. Open your Google Sheet—your test submission is already there!
  4. Open your Formdata dashboard to inspect submission details, user-agent data, and CSV export options.
