Webflow & Framer

  1. Click on your Form block on your page.
  2. In Form Settings, find Action / URL.
  3. Paste your Endpoint URL.
  4. Set the HTTP Method to POST.
  5. Make sure each input field has a Name.

Carrd & Squarespace

  1. Select your Form element.
  2. Set Type/Destination to Custom URL.
  3. Paste your Endpoint URL.
  4. Set Method to POST.
  5. Publish your site and send a test message.

WordPress

  1. Edit your page or post in WordPress.
  2. Add a Custom HTML block.
  3. Select the WordPress tab below and click Copy code.
  4. Paste into the block and click Publish!

How Spam Protection Works

Keep your form safe from bots without bothering your visitors.

✓

Automatic Honeypot (Zero Setup)

A hidden form field ({{ form.honeypot or '_gotcha' }}) is included in your code. Real human visitors cannot see it, but automatic spam bots always fill it in. When Formdata detects a value in this field, the submission is rejected instantly.

Active by default • 100% free • Invisible to humans
∞

Cloudflare Turnstile (Optional)

Cloudflare Turnstile provides smart, invisible bot challenges without asking visitors to solve annoying puzzles. Enable it anytime in your Settings → Spam protection tab by adding your free Cloudflare credentials.

Optional • Great for high-traffic sites

Helpful Tips & FAQ

Input Field Names

Always include a name="..." on your inputs (e.g. name="email"). This allows Formdata to organize your columns.

File Uploads

To receive resume or photo attachments, simply add enctype="multipart/form-data" to your <form> tag.

Custom Thank You Page

Want to redirect visitors to your own custom thank you page after submitting? Configure your redirect URL under Settings → Redirects.
