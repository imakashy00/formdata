Form backend for builders 👷

Add forms to any site.
No backend needed.

Point your HTML form at a Formdata endpoint and get submissions straight to your inbox — with spam filtering, file uploads, Slack alerts, and a full dashboard.

contact.html
<form action="https://formdata.cloud/f/form-id"
     method="POST">

  <input type="text" name="name"/>
  <input type="email" name="email"/>
  <textarea name="message"></textarea>

  <button type="submit">Send</button>
</form>
Submissions arrive in your dashboard instantly
