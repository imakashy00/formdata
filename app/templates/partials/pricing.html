How Formdata Integrates with Google

Formdata integrates directly with Google Sheets to help builders automate their workflows. When you authorize Formdata to connect with your Google Account, we use this access solely to send and append new form submissions to a Google Sheet of your choice. We do not access, store, or share any other personal files or data from your Google Drive. You can disconnect this integration at any time from your Formdata dashboard.
