HTML form explanation for beginners:

  • action: Points to your unique Formdata endpoint where messages are saved securely.
  • name attributes: Each input must have a name="..." (e.g. name, email, message). This becomes the column title in your Submissions table.
  • honeypot: The hidden input ({{ form.honeypot or '_gotcha' }}) is invisible to people, but tricks spam bots into filling it out so we can automatically reject spam.
