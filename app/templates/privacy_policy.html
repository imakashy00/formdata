1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • Visitors to formdata.cloud.
  • People who create or use a Formdata account.
  • Customers who use Formdata to process form submissions.
  • People who submit information through a Formdata-powered form.
  • People who communicate with Formdata support.

For information submitted through a customer's form, the customer may determine the purposes and means of processing. In those situations, Formdata generally acts as a service provider or processor on the customer's behalf.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create a Formdata account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Password or authentication credentials.
  • Account and workspace information.
  • Billing and subscription information.
  • Communication preferences.

2.2 Form and Submission Data

When you use Formdata to receive submissions, our systems may process information submitted through your forms, including:

  • Names.
  • Email addresses.
  • Phone numbers.
  • Messages.
  • Form fields and responses.
  • Files and attachments.
  • Metadata associated with submissions.
  • IP addresses and other technical information associated with requests.

The actual information contained in a submission depends on what the Formdata customer chooses to collect.

Customer responsibility:

Customers should only collect information that they have a lawful basis and appropriate authorization to collect. Customers are responsible for providing their own notices to people submitting information through their forms where required by law.

2.3 Usage and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information when you interact with our website and services, such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type.
  • Operating system.
  • Device information.
  • Pages visited.
  • Referring website.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Error and diagnostic information.
  • Service performance information.

We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Formdata.

2.4 Payment Information

Paid subscriptions are processed through our payment provider. We generally do not store complete payment card numbers on our own servers. Payment information is handled by the applicable payment processor according to its own privacy policy and security practices. We may receive limited billing information such as:

  • Subscription status.
  • Transaction identifiers.
  • Billing email.
  • Plan information.
  • Payment status.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate Formdata.
  • Process form submissions.
  • Store submissions and uploaded files.
  • Send email notifications requested by customers.
  • Deliver integrations and webhooks configured by customers.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Monitor service reliability.
  • Detect spam, abuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • Prevent unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve our products and services.
  • Communicate important service or account information.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell customer submission data as a product or use customer submission content for unrelated advertising purposes.

4. Form Submissions and Customer Data

Formdata is designed to process data submitted through forms on behalf of our customers. For example, if a business creates a contact form using Formdata and a visitor submits their name and email address, Formdata processes that submission so the business can access and use it.

The customer operating that form is generally responsible for:

  • Determining what information to collect.
  • Establishing the appropriate legal basis for collection.
  • Providing appropriate privacy notices.
  • Responding to requests from people whose data they collect.
  • Configuring retention and deletion appropriately.
  • Ensuring their use of Formdata complies with applicable law.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping users authenticated.
  • Maintaining sessions.
  • Remembering preferences.
  • Security.
  • Analytics.
  • Understanding how our website and service are used.

You can configure your browser to reject certain cookies. Some Formdata functionality may not work correctly if essential cookies are disabled.

6. Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help operate Formdata. These providers may assist with:

  • Cloud hosting.
  • Database infrastructure.
  • File storage.
  • Email delivery.
  • Payment processing.
  • Authentication.
  • Monitoring.
  • Security.
  • Analytics.
  • Customer support.

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services and are expected to protect that information appropriately.

7. Integrations

Formdata allows customers to connect services such as email, Slack, webhooks, spreadsheets, or other third-party systems. When a customer enables an integration, submission information may be transmitted to that third party according to the customer's configuration. Formdata is not responsible for how an external integration provider subsequently uses information after it leaves Formdata's systems.

7.1. Google API Services User Data Policy Disclosure

Formdata's integration with Google API Services (such as Google OAuth) allows us to access certain Google user data with your explicit authorization.

  • Data Accessed: We only request the minimum required permissions (scopes) needed to integrate our service with your Google Account, such as exporting form submissions to Google Sheets or authenticating your account using Google Sign-In.
  • Data Use: We use this data strictly to provide and improve user-facing features within Formdata. We do not use Google user data for any other purposes.
  • Data Sharing and Transfer: We do not sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, including advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • Compliance & Limited Use: Formdata's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures may include:

  • Encryption in transit.
  • Access controls.
  • Authentication mechanisms.
  • Logging and monitoring.
  • Infrastructure security controls.
  • Backup and recovery mechanisms.
  • Security reviews and operational safeguards.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting customer information, we will take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, and respond to the incident and provide notices where required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the service.
  • Maintain customer accounts.
  • Fulfill contractual obligations.
  • Meet legal requirements.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Enforce agreements.
  • Maintain security and abuse-prevention records.

Customers are responsible for managing the retention of submissions within their accounts where Formdata provides deletion or management functionality. After an account is terminated, we may retain certain information for a limited period for legitimate business, legal, security, backup, or recovery purposes.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including:

  • Accessing personal information.
  • Requesting correction.
  • Requesting deletion.
  • Requesting restriction of processing.
  • Objecting to certain processing.
  • Requesting data portability.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.

If you submitted information through a form operated by a Formdata customer, your request may need to be directed to that customer because they determine why your information was collected.

11. International Data Transfers

Formdata may use service providers or infrastructure located in countries other than your own. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

Formdata is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently consent to online services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating Formdata accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we may provide notice through our website, account dashboard, email, or another reasonable method. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when it was most recently revised.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us through the contact information provided on formdata.cloud.
