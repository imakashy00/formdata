# Formdata

> Formdata is a form backend for HTML, static websites, Webflow, Framer, React, and JavaScript applications. It lets developers collect form submissions without building or maintaining their own backend.

## Product

Formdata provides a backend for websites and applications that need to receive and manage form submissions.

Key capabilities include:
- Form submission collection
- Spam and bot protection
- Email notifications
- Submission management and export
- Integrations with services such as Google Sheets, Notion, and Slack
- API-based form submission handling
- Rate limiting and submission protection

## Who is Formdata for?

Formdata is designed for:
- Developers building static HTML websites
- Webflow and Framer users
- React and JavaScript applications
- Landing pages and marketing websites
- Websites that need forms without building a custom backend
- Peole who want to add contact us form in their static page

## How it works

A developer creates a form in their website and submits the form data to Formdata. Formdata processes and stores the submission and provides tools for managing submissions and integrations.

Formdata does not require the customer's website to run its own server-side form-processing backend.
